# Android APK Installation Guide

Install `OpenRecipeBook` directly on an Android phone or tablet using the signed `.apk` package.

## Safety & Authenticity Warning

- **Official `main` Releases Only**: Download `OpenRecipeBook-Android.apk` exclusively from [DontKwo/OpenRecipeBook Releases](https://github.com/DontKwo/OpenRecipeBook/releases/latest).
- **Fork Warning**: Do **not** install APKs from forked repositories or third-party mirrors unless you have verified the source code yourself and confirmed they contain no malware. Only official `main` releases are maintained by the author, and I make no promises.
- **Bypassing Security Prompts**: Android warns whenever you install an APK downloaded outside Google Play (and may label unknown apps "unsafe" or blocked by Google Play Protect). Bypassing these prompts is strictly at your own discretion, and `OpenRecipeBook` is provided "AS IS" with zero liability.

---

## Step-by-Step Android Installation

1. On your Android device, open the [Latest Releases](https://github.com/DontKwo/OpenRecipeBook/releases/latest) page and download `OpenRecipeBook-Android.apk`.
2. Tap the downloaded `OpenRecipeBook-Android.apk` notification (or open your phone's **Files** app -> **Downloads** and tap the APK).
3. When Android asks to allow installing unknown apps from your browser or file manager:
   - Tap **Settings** in the prompt.
   - Turn on **Allow from this source** for that browser or file manager.
   - Return to the installer and tap **Install**.
4. If **Google Play Protect** displays *"Unsafe app blocked"* or *"App not recognized"*:
   - Tap **More details** -> **Install anyway** (or allow Play Protect to **Scan app** first if offered).
   - Proceed strictly at your own discretion after confirming the APK came from `DontKwo/OpenRecipeBook`.
5. **Samsung Galaxy (One UI 6+ Auto Blocker Note)**:
   - If installation is blocked completely with *"For your security, your phone isn't allowed to install unknown apps"*, open **Settings** -> **Security and privacy** -> **Auto Blocker** and turn it off temporarily while installing, or review Samsung App Protection settings. Re-enable Auto Blocker afterward if desired.

## Updating OpenRecipeBook on Android

Download the newer `OpenRecipeBook-Android.apk` from [Latest Releases](https://github.com/DontKwo/OpenRecipeBook/releases/latest) and tap **Update**. Your recipes and settings stay intact across official `main` releases signed with the same key.
